## Story choice rule (from 2026-10-24)
Mike noticed many accounts retell the same famous stories (Eiffel Tower con, Ponzi, Capone) with no views. So:
- PREFER little-known true stories that most viewers have never heard of. Skip the "top 20 most retold" stories
  (Ponzi, Lustig/Eiffel, Capone, Madoff, Escobar, Frank Abagnale, Bonnie & Clyde, D.B. Cooper, Enron, Blockbuster/Netflix).
- If a famous person is used, tell a lesser-known episode of their life, not the usual story.
- The hook must work for someone who has never heard of the person: lead with the absurd fact, not the name.
